# Naming Conventions

Use readable, stable filenames with lowercase words separated by hyphens.

Examples:

- `Wiki/Topics/llm-wiki.md`
- `Wiki/Concepts/compiled-knowledge.md`
- `Wiki/Entities/obsidian.md`
- `Wiki/Projects/ai-brain.md`
- `Wiki/Logs/llm-wiki-build-log.md`
- `Raw/Sources/how-to-build-llm-wiki-in-obsidian.md`

Prefer durable names over clever names. A future agent should understand the note's purpose from its path.
