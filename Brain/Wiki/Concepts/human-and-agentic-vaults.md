# Human And Agentic Vaults

## Definition

Human and agentic vault separation keeps personal thinking distinct from AI-generated or AI-maintained knowledge.

## Key Points

- A human vault can preserve the user's own thinking.
- An agentic vault can hold compiled knowledge that AI tools maintain and query.
- Separation makes it easier to know which notes came from the user and which were produced or reorganized by AI.
- This project currently uses [[ai-brain-obsidian-vault|AI Brain Obsidian Vault]] as the agentic LLM Wiki.
